*Checked at Apply, once the target is computed.*

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *PSUs smaller than the cluster size (…): … PSU(s) cannot host a cluster* | lower the cluster size, or remove these PSUs from the frame |
| *Insufficient stratum capacity: … stratum(s) cannot reach their target with clusters of …* | lower the cluster size or the target, or merge PSUs |

The full list of the PSUs or strata concerned can be downloaded from the message.
