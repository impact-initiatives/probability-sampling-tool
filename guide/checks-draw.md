*Checked when Sample is clicked.*

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Draw blocked: … PSU(s) were selected several times and would need more surveys than their population* | lower the cluster size or the target, or merge PSUs; another seed may also avoid it |

A **warning**, not a block, *Sample size exceeds population*, appears after Apply and after Sample when the requested size exceeds a stratum in simple random or PPS sampling: the draw is capped to the population.
